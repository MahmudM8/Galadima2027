Meet the candidate

Ahmed Tijjani Galadima Aminu — a public servant rooted in Adamawa

Born on 26 February 1964 in Yola, into the family of the late Galadima of Adamawa, Ahmed Tijjani Galadima Aminu carries that inheritance of responsibility with humility — and he chose a career of service.

He built his reputation at the Petroleum Technology Development Fund (PTDF), joining in its formative years and rising to Head of Education and later General Manager of the Education Department. In those roles, he helped run scholarship programmes that sent young Nigerians to study at home and abroad. When the national economy tightened, he arranged free-tuition partnerships with foreign universities so that scholars could continue their studies — the resourcefulness of an administrator who protects a mission when budgets shrink.

President Tinubu later appointed him Executive Secretary and CEO of PTDF. Under his leadership, PTDF promoted the College of Petroleum and Energy Studies Kaduna, a postgraduate institution meant to close the skills gap in the energy sector. The College has since signed a partnership with Robert Gordon University to launch a new energy doctoral programme in Nigeria.

He is a man of quiet discipline who spent years in a competitive scholarship system without any publicly documented allegation of misconduct against him. When the time came, he resigned from PTDF to seek the APC governorship ticket. Three aspirants withdrew in his favour before the primary, and he won with 414,444 votes.
